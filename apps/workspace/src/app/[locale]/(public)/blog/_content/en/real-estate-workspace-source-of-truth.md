Real estate teams lose time when project status, unit availability, client history, and approvals live in separate tools. A workspace source of truth reduces that drift by making the operational record easy to find and hard to bypass.

## What belongs in the workspace

The workspace should hold the information that changes daily and affects team decisions:

- Project and unit status
- Client requirements and follow-up history
- Viewing schedules
- Media and documents
- Team permissions
- Approved partner access

When this data stays connected, brokers and developers can answer practical questions without waiting for another spreadsheet or chat thread.

## Why partner access matters

Modern real estate teams use external tools for automation, AI assistance, messaging, and reporting. Those tools should not receive broad permanent access. A workspace should approve partner access by organization, scope, and status so every integration can be reviewed and removed when needed.

## The operating benefit

A clean workspace source of truth gives teams fewer duplicate updates, faster handoffs, and a clearer audit trail. The goal is not another dashboard. The goal is one reliable operational record that the team can use every day.
