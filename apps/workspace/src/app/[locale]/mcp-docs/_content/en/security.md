---

Before connecting an agent, confirm its client identity, organization, scope, permissions, and expiry.

## Security Best Practices

* **Use narrow scope**: Prefer selected spaces or projects over organization-wide access.
* **Grant exact actions**: Enable writes or deletes only when the workflow requires them.
* **Use short expiry**: Prefer 7 or 30 days for temporary agents.
* **Revoke immediately**: Revocation and permission loss are checked on the next request.
