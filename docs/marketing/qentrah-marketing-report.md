## 1. Executive Summary

Qentrah is the **operating system for real estate teams** — a unified, AI-powered workspace platform that eliminates the scattered tools, manual processes, and lost deals that cost brokers and developers millions every year.

Built for the Saudi Arabia and GCC real estate market, Qentrah brings together:
- A full **Work OS** (clients, opportunities, projects, tasks, calendar, assets)
- An **AI agent layer** with WhatsApp, Slack, and Telegram integrations
- A **partner app ecosystem** for third-party real estate tools
- A native **mobile experience** for brokers on the move
- Bilingual (Arabic / English) first-class support

The platform targets real estate brokers, developers, and sales teams who are currently operating across WhatsApp, Excel, disconnected CRMs, and manual signing processes — and replaces all of it with one intelligent, automated workspace.

## 2. Product Overview

### 2.1 What Is Qentrah?

Qentrah is a **multi-app SaaS platform** organized around a central Workspace product that owns all business data. It is structured as:

| App | Who It's For | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Brokers, sales teams, real estate orgs | Main product — CRM, project mgmt, assets, tasks, calendar, AI, billing |
| **Partners Portal** | Third-party app developers | Register, test, and publish integrations into Qentrah |
| **Admin Review** | Internal Qentrah operators | Review and approve partner app submissions |
| **Mobile** | Field agents, brokers | On-the-go property, assistant, voice, and listing flows |
| **Marketing Site** | Prospects | Public-facing homepage, pricing, legal, contact |

### 2.2 Core Product Capabilities

#### Work OS (Core)
- **Client Management** — Full client profiles, relationship tracking, contact history
- **Opportunity Pipeline** — Lead-to-close stage tracking with custom fields and automation rules
- **Project Management** — Developer project records with asset inventory, timelines, and milestones
- **Asset Management** — Individual unit records with pricing, availability, media, and linked opportunities
- **Task Management** — Team task assignment, due dates, status tracking
- **Calendar** — Viewing appointments, follow-up reminders, event scheduling
- **Automation Rules** — Triggers on record creation, stage changes, due dates that auto-create tasks, schedule events, send notifications

#### AI Layer
- **AI Agents** — Embedded AI that assists with triage, drafting, summaries, and repetitive operational work
- **MCP Tools** — Machine-callable tools exposing workspace data for AI workflows
- **AI Credit Cards** — Bundled AI usage quotas included with Better plan and above
- **OpenRouter integration** — Flexible AI model routing

#### Integration & API Platform
- **Partner App Ecosystem** — OAuth-based authorization allowing third-party tools to access Workspace data with user consent
- **WhatsApp** — Client follow-ups, viewing reminders, lead replies (2.4-second pipeline)
- **Slack** — Team alerts, approvals, project updates
- **Telegram** — Operational messages and fast internal routing
- **Google Sheets** — Inventory imports, reports, exports
- **Airtable** — Structured lists, review queues, team workflows
- **Notion** — Briefs, launch notes, operating playbooks
- **Zapier** — No-code automations between Qentrah and existing stacks

#### Mobile (Expo)
- Property search and listing flows
- AI assistant and voice interface
- Map-based decision layer
- Preferences and account management

#### Billing & Payments
- **Tamara BNPL** — Buy Now Pay Later for annual plans (Saudi-market exclusive)
- Monthly and annual billing cycles
- Per-market pricing (Saudi Arabia first)

## 3. Target Market

### 3.1 Primary Segment: Saudi Real Estate Operations Teams

**Who they are:**
- Real estate brokers and agencies (5–200 person teams)
- Real estate developers with sales teams
- Property management companies
- Real estate investment companies with active deal flow

**Where they are today:**
- Managing clients over WhatsApp group chats
- Tracking inventory in Excel or Google Sheets
- Using fragmented, non-real-estate-specific CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot) or none at all
- Signing contracts via PDF email chains or manual processes
- Losing qualified leads because no one responded fast enough

**The pain they feel:**
1. **Scattered Data** — Pricing and inventory exist in disconnected chats, spreadsheets, and old CRMs. Manual copy-errors and constant pricing mismatches destroy deals.
2. **Slow Execution** — Contracts wait for manual signatures. Price confirmations take days of redundant messaging.
3. **Lost Opportunities** — Qualified clients expire or move to faster competitors because brokers lack instant, updated asset availability.

**Why they choose Qentrah:**
- One source of truth for all data
- 2.4-second pipeline: AI verifies asset details and dispatches WhatsApp agreements for e-signature in seconds
- Zero lead leakage: clients get interactive brochures within 30 seconds, even outside business hours
- Arabic-first, Saudi-market-native experience
- Tamara BNPL for predictable annual cash flow

### 3.2 Secondary Segment: GCC Real Estate Markets

- UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Egypt
- Similar operational challenges, expanding after Saudi traction
- Localization-ready with i18n architecture (Arabic/English bilingual)

### 3.3 Tertiary Segment: Real Estate Tech Developers (Partners)

- Third-party SaaS companies building tools for GCC real estate
- Integrate with Qentrah via the Partners portal
- Access Workspace resource APIs through OAuth authorization
- Expand product value through partner app catalog

4. **WhatsApp-first operations** — where real estate in KSA actually runs
5. **Tamara BNPL** — unique Saudi-market payment flexibility for annual plans
6. **Bilingual Arabic/English** — RTL-first design, not an afterthought
7. **Mobile + web parity** — brokers work from their phone; Qentrah is built for it
