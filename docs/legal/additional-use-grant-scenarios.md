# Additional Use Grant scenario matrix

This matrix tests the intended reading of Qentrah v0.1.0's BSL Additional Use Grant. It is a drafting aid, not legal advice, and every expected result remains subject to counsel approval.

| Scenario | Expected result under the draft | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal production use with no commercial beneficiary | Permitted | Personal production use is expressly included. |
| Classroom, education, or academic research | Permitted | Educational and research uses are expressly included. |
| Nonprofit production deployment | Permitted | Charitable and nonprofit purposes are expressly included when not primarily for commercial advantage. |
| Government department deployment | Permitted | Governmental purposes are expressly included. |
| Affiliated corporate group with prior-year consolidated worldwide gross revenue below USD 1,000,000 | Permitted | The entire affiliated group qualifies for Small Business Use. |
| Affiliated corporate group at or above USD 1,000,000 | Separate commercial license required | The consolidated group is outside the small-business threshold. |
| Small consultant operating Qentrah for a large customer | Separate commercial license required | Both the service provider group and ultimate beneficiary group must independently qualify. |
| Low-revenue subsidiary of a large parent | Separate commercial license required | Affiliate revenue is consolidated; subsidiaries cannot avoid the threshold. |
| Multiple deployments split among affiliates or customers | No threshold avoidance | Revenue and use cannot be separated by deployment, subsidiary, customer, or geography. |
| Internal evaluation, testing, or proof of concept that is not production use | Permitted | BSL 1.1 permits non-production use. |
| Hosted production service offered to nonqualifying customers | Separate commercial license required | The grant excludes a service substantially derived from Qentrah for a nonqualifying beneficiary. |

Counsel must confirm edge cases including mixed nonprofit/commercial use, fiscal-year conversions, joint ventures, franchises, public-private partnerships, contractors, and changes of control before publication.
