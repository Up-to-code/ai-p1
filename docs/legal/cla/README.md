# Qentrah Contributor License Agreements

These Harmony-based CLA drafts preserve contributor ownership while granting
Ahmed Mansour the copyright, patent, sublicensing, and relicensing rights
needed to distribute Qentrah under BSL, commercial terms, and future Open
Source terms.

- Individuals sign `individual.md`.
- Authorized representatives contributing entity-owned work sign `entity.md`.
- CLA Assistant supplies the electronic acceptance record and collects only
  the fields described in `privacy.md`.

The forms are based on Harmony Contributor License Agreement templates 1.0,
available at <https://www.harmonyagreements.org/agreements> and licensed under
CC BY 3.0.

## Hard gate

These documents are drafts pending legal review. Do not enable CLA Assistant,
accept a signature, or merge an external contribution until counsel approves
the forms and the approval is recorded in the publication checklist.
