# CLA Assistant privacy notice

After legal approval, Qentrah plans to use the hosted CLA Assistant service to
authenticate signers through GitHub and enforce a required pull-request status
check.

The service will be configured to collect only:

- full name, email address, and GitHub account;
- agreement version, acceptance time, and signer category;
- for entity signatures, legal entity name, address, signer title, and covered
  contributor accounts; and
- for individual signatures, whether an employer owns the contribution.

The CLA Assistant project states that hosted signature records are stored in
an Azure-hosted database in Europe and may be exported by repository owners.
Qentrah will restrict exports to maintainers handling legal compliance, retain
them for as long as contributions remain in the project, and use them only to
document contribution rights and resolve related disputes.

Questions and data requests go to `legal@qentrah.com`. This notice and the
service's current privacy terms must be reviewed by counsel before activation.
