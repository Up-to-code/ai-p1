Principles

Communication First
Every component feels like a node in a communication network. Agents, humans, data, and automation are visualized as connected entities.
Apple Minimalism
Near-black primary in light mode, pure white in dark. Warm off-white backgrounds. Extreme restraint in decoration.
Calm Technology
Invisible complexity. The interface recedes, letting the communication and orchestration take center stage.
