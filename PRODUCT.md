# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mino Design serves real estate operators first: brokers, sales teams, coordinators, admins, and organization owners who manage clients, properties, projects, inventory readiness, approvals, and daily follow-up work.

These users move between public trust surfaces and dense product surfaces. They need Arabic and English experiences that feel equally considered, with RTL layouts treated as first-class design input rather than a translation pass.

The default register is product because the repo's core value is operational work. Public landing, marketing, docs, and partner trust pages may override into brand register when design needs to carry persuasion, storytelling, or identity.

## Product Purpose

Mino Design is the repo-wide design language for the Anan monorepo. It should make every surface feel like one composed Saudi real estate operating system, from the public workspace landing page to dashboards, OAuth consent, partner review, admin surfaces, and reusable UI packages.

The product exists to help real estate teams see what matters today, act on client and inventory work, trust shared data, and move between human operations and platform automation without visual friction.

Success means a team member can open any Mino surface and immediately understand priority, ownership, state, and next action. The interface should reduce coordination drag, not advertise complexity.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, premium.

The voice is confident and spare. It should sound like an expert operations partner: clear about status, careful with risk, direct about actions, and never theatrical inside task-heavy product UI.

Brand surfaces may become more cinematic than product surfaces, but they should inherit the same sense of control, clarity, and quiet authority visible in the workspace landing page.

## Anti-references

Do not turn Mino into a stock Google Material clone. Avoid default Material component rhythms, generic enterprise admin layouts, and surfaces that feel assembled from off-the-shelf UI templates.

Also avoid generic SaaS tropes: purple-blue gradients as identity, endless icon-card grids, nested cards, glassmorphism as default, gradient text, vague AI-startup styling, hero metric templates, and luxury real estate cliches such as black-and-gold brochure styling, marble surfaces, or empty prestige.

Mino should not feel like a decorative landing page pasted onto an operational product. It should not bury primary work under ornament, motion, or marketing copy.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with operational priority. Every screen should make the next useful action clearer than the surrounding chrome.
2. Keep trust visible. Permissions, approvals, data freshness, and risk states need explicit, legible treatment.
3. Treat Arabic as native. RTL composition, Cairo typography, and Arabic copy density must be designed, not merely mirrored.
4. Use brand energy selectively. Public and landing surfaces can carry cinematic atmosphere; product workflows should stay calm and fast.
5. Make the system feel authored. Mino should have consistent tokens, components, and visual rules, but should not collapse into generic framework defaults.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA as the baseline across product and brand surfaces. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic structure, touch targets, responsive behavior, and readable contrast.

Support reduced motion. Motion should clarify state, reveal hierarchy, or confirm action; it should never block work or become the only way to understand state.

Design for Arabic and English parity, long text, short labels, dynamic user content, data-heavy lists, empty states, loading states, error states, and narrow mobile viewports.
