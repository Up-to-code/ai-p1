Manage your entire client lifecycle seamlessly: **Clients → Opportunities → Projects → Tasks**. All in one place. All with AI working alongside your team.

## 3. How It Works: Client → Opportunity → Project → Task

Qentrah is designed around the way agencies actually work:

1. **Clients & Contacts:** Your client relationships live in one place. Full history, contact details, communication timeline, and all linked opportunities and projects.

2. **Opportunities (Sales Pipeline):** Wins become projects with a single click. Opportunities live alongside your clients, showing pipeline value, stage, and probability. When you close a deal, AI can automatically create a project using your agency-specific templates.

3. **Projects (Delivery Container):** Where the real work happens. Each project is a fully-featured container with:
   - **Overview:** Project health, timeline, budget, margin, status at a glance
   - **Tasks:** Kanban or list view; AI suggests task creation and assignment from project context
   - **Team:** Who's assigned, workload visibility, capacity planning
   - **Files:** Assets, deliverables, documents—organized and searchable
   - **Calendar:** Project-scoped timeline, milestones, and deadlines
   - **Activity:** Full log of changes, decisions, and conversations

4. **Tasks:** The atomic unit of work. Assigned to team members, tracked in pipelines, updated automatically by AI when relevant events trigger (project status changes, deadlines approaching, dependent work completed).

5. **Global Workspace Mode:** Step back and see everything. Dashboard with AI chat, client overviews, pipeline summary, top projects, team capacity, and AI-suggested next actions across all work.

6. **Project Mode:** Zoom in. Seamless switching via top bar project selector to focus on a single project with all its tabs, context, and deep work.

Throughout, **AI is working**: creating projects, suggesting task assignments, surfacing project risks, predicting timeline issues, auto-summarizing client activity, and proactively recommending next steps so your team stays ahead.

- **Rich Project Interface:** Overview, Tasks, Team, Files, Calendar, Activity tabs

### Tasks & Workflows
- **Flexible Task Views:** Kanban boards, list views, timeline view

- **Agentic Operator:** AI doesn't just chat—it actively manages your workspace
- **Project Creation from Opportunities:** Win deal → AI creates project from template with auto-populated client, timeline, team

- **Proactive Next Actions:** AI surfaces what needs attention: overdue tasks, budget concerns, missed deadlines, approaching deliverables

### Calendar & Timeline

- **Universal Search:** Find clients, opportunities, projects, tasks, conversations, files—all instantly

## 5. Who We Built This For

Qentrah is built for agencies and professional service firms that are tired of tool sprawl and admin overhead:

1. **Marketing Agencies:** Managing multiple campaigns per client, tracking deliverables, coordinating creative + strategy teams. Need to see profitability per campaign and team capacity at a glance.

2. **Creative & Design Studios:** Project-heavy workflows with lots of file assets, revision cycles, and team collaboration. Need templates for different project types (brand design, website redesign, packaging, etc.) and clear timeline visibility.

3. **Web Development & Digital Agencies:** Juggling design, development, QA, and client feedback phases. Need task workflows, milestone tracking, and the ability to see which projects are on budget and which are draining resources.

4. **Consulting & Strategy Firms:** Retainer engagements mixed with project work. Need to track billable hours, understand engagement profitability, and manage overlapping client relationships with the same team.

5. **Recruitment & Staffing Firms:** Multiple placements per candidate, multiple clients per role, busy pipelines. Need to see active opportunities, candidate pipelines, client feedback, and placement profitability.

We believe that powerful technology should not come with a steep learning curve. Qentrah feels intuitive from day one—no complex commands, no feature bloat. New team members are productive in their first hour, not their first week.

Aesthetics are a core feature. Qentrah is designed to be beautiful—micro-interactions that delight, thoughtful typography, generous spacing, a clutter-free interface. You actually *enjoy* spending time in your workspace. The simplicity hides immense complexity; the design makes it feel effortless.

Every screen is optimized for speed—no unnecessary clicks, no slow loads. Keyboard shortcuts for power users. Accessibility standards met without compromise. Your team's time is valuable; Qentrah respects that.
