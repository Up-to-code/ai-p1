standard BSL terms permit non-production use, and Qentrah's Additional Use
Grant permits the production uses described in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

A separate commercial license is required when production use falls outside
that grant. Typical examples include:

- production use by an organization whose worldwide affiliated group earned
  USD 1,000,000 or more in gross revenue during the preceding fiscal year;
- operating Qentrah for a customer that does not independently qualify for the
  Additional Use Grant;
- reselling, hosting, or providing a service substantially derived from
  Qentrah to a nonqualifying customer; or
- requesting different warranty, support, indemnity, branding, or distribution
  terms.

Pricing and contract terms are negotiated privately. Contact
[legal@qentrah.com](mailto:legal@qentrah.com) with the legal name of the user,
the intended deployment, the number of organizations or customers served, and
the most recent consolidated annual gross revenue band.
